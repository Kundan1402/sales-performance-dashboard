# Business Summary

**Overall:** Revenue is 3,252,817 with a profit margin of 26.1% across 5,000 orders.

## Key insights
1. Revenue grew 17.4% in the latest year compared with the year before.
2. **North** is the top region by revenue. **North** has the best profit margin.
3. **Laptop** is the best-selling product by revenue.
4. Sales peak in **Dec** and are lowest in **Sep**.

## Recommendations
- Stock more inventory and run campaigns before **Dec**, when demand is highest.
- Push marketing in regions with lower revenue to grow them.
